THE PRODUCT

Meet Workplace Connect.

One private network for the people inside your organisation — designed around real employee needs, not another corporate feed.

01

Meet

Help employees discover colleagues they would not normally cross paths with — for coffee, lunch, a virtual conversation or a shared interest.

02

Help

Surface people who can guide someone through an internal tool, process, technology or business challenge based on volunteered expertise.

03

Learn

Connect employees with peers, mentors and experienced colleagues who can help them build knowledge, skills and confidence.

04

Belong

Make a large organisation feel more human by helping people discover colleagues beyond their immediate team, office or existing network.

A DIFFERENT APPROACH

Not another employee directory.
Not another social feed.

Most workplace systems tell employees where people sit. Ligeronex focuses on a more useful question:

“I need a person. Find me the right one.”

Workplace Connect is built to become an always-available human-discovery layer for the enterprise — helping employees act on a need in the moment.
