AI should help people connect — not expose them.

Workplace Connect is designed around organisation boundaries, employee choice and privacy-conscious matching. The MVP uses corporate email + OTP access and tenant-scoped sessions, with stronger enterprise identity integrations able to follow as the platform matures.

FAQ

Questions organisations may ask.

Is Workplace Connect another employee social network?

No. It is designed around employee intent — finding a useful person for a specific need — rather than maintaining a social feed.

How does matching work?

The product is designed to combine intent with relevant profile signals such as expertise, interests, goals, experience, role context, location and preferences. The exact weighting can evolve by use case.

Can organisations support mentoring and onboarding?

Yes, these are natural future use cases for the platform. The product architecture is being designed so different connection intents can use different matching logic.

Does AI make the final decision?

No. AI assists discovery. Employees remain in control of who they choose to connect with, and organisations can define appropriate boundaries and policies.
