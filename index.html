01What is Ligeronex?+

Ligeronex is building practical AI products for organisations that want to make information clearer, work more intelligent and create better human experiences. Our philosophy is simple: Light. Intelligence. Beyond.

02What problem is Ligeronex trying to solve?+

Organisations have more information and technology than ever, yet people still face fragmented systems, repetitive processes and disconnected experiences. We are focused on turning AI into useful products that reduce that friction.

03What exactly are you launching?+

We are intentionally keeping the product details private ahead of the reveal. What we can share is the direction: practical AI, connected intelligence, human-centred experiences and measurable organisational value. The product portfolio will be revealed on 18 December 2026.

04Who is Ligeronex building for?+

Our focus is organisations dealing with complex information, workflows and decision-making. We are particularly interested in environments where better intelligence can improve how people work, collaborate and act.

05What makes Ligeronex different from another AI company?+

We are not building AI for the sake of AI. Our approach is product-led and problem-first: start with a meaningful organisational problem, apply the right intelligence, keep humans in control and build something people can actually use.

06How will Ligeronex make money?+

Our commercial model will be designed around the products and organisations we serve, with recurring software economics where appropriate. Pricing and commercial details will be shared as the products move closer to market.

07Why is now the right time to build this company?+

AI capabilities are moving rapidly from experimentation into everyday business workflows. The opportunity is shifting from simply accessing models to designing reliable products around them — with context, governance, usability and measurable outcomes.

08How do you think about responsible AI, privacy and security?+

Trust is part of the product. We are designing around human control, responsible use of AI, privacy-conscious information handling and security from the beginning rather than treating them as features added later.

09What is the long-term vision?+

To build a portfolio of practical AI products that becomes a trusted intelligence layer for modern organisations — helping people see more clearly, decide better and move beyond traditional ways of working.

10What evidence will investors be able to evaluate?+

As we approach launch, we will share the information appropriate to the stage of the company, including product direction, customer problem validation, commercial model, market opportunity and the indicators we use to measure progress.

11What is the growth opportunity?+

We see an opportunity to build beyond a single AI application: a portfolio of focused products that can address recurring organisational needs while sharing a common philosophy, technology foundation and trust model.

12Are you looking for customers, partners or investors?+

Yes — we are interested in thoughtful conversations with organisations, technology partners and investors who understand the opportunity in practical, human-centred AI. If the direction resonates with you, start a conversation.
